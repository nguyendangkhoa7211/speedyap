# SpeedYap for Mac

Spin a random word, talk about it for two minutes, and get quicker, clearer and more creative at speaking. Ollie the otter keeps your streak, scores your filler words and pace, and hands out stickers.

## Download
1. Go to **[Releases → Latest](../../releases/latest)** and download `SpeedYap-x.y.dmg`.
2. Open the DMG and drag **SpeedYap** into **Applications**.
3. Open SpeedYap. First time only: if macOS says it can't verify the app, click **Done**, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

SpeedYap updates itself automatically. You can also use **SpeedYap → Check for Updates…**

Requires macOS 26 or later. Recordings and transcripts stay on your Mac.
